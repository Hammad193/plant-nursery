About This Plant

Bring Tropical Character Into Your Home

Monstera Deliciosa is a popular indoor plant known for its large, naturally split leaves and strong tropical appearance. It can create a beautiful focal point in living rooms, offices, bedrooms and other bright indoor spaces.

With suitable indirect light, moderate watering and good drainage, this plant can grow into a striking addition to your indoor plant collection. Its bold foliage works well with modern, minimal and natural interior styles.

Light

Place the plant near bright indirect light. Avoid harsh direct sunlight for long periods.

Watering

Water when the upper soil layer becomes dry. Avoid leaving excess water around the roots.

Humidity

Moderate humidity can support healthy tropical foliage and fresh looking leaves.

Pruning

Remove damaged or yellow leaves regularly to keep the plant neat and encourage healthy growth.
