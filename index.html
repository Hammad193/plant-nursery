Frequently Asked Questions

Plant Nursery Questions Answered

Find helpful answers about plants, plant care, delivery, gardening services, repotting, and choosing the right plants for your space.

Green Haven Nursery offers a wide selection of indoor plants, outdoor plants, flowering plants, decorative plants, and greenery suitable for homes, offices, balconies, and gardens.

Yes. We provide indoor plants that can add natural beauty to living rooms, bedrooms, workspaces, and other indoor areas. Plant selection can be based on available light and space.

Beginner friendly plants are generally those that can handle normal indoor conditions and do not require complicated care. Our team can help you select plants according to your experience, available light, and daily routine.

Watering frequency depends on the plant type, pot size, soil condition, temperature, and available light. Check the soil before watering and avoid keeping the roots constantly soaked.

Yes. We can help you choose plants based on room size, sunlight, humidity, maintenance requirements, and the overall look you want to create in your home, office, or garden.

Yes. Some plants can perform well in lower light conditions. Our team can recommend suitable options after considering the natural light available in your room or workspace.

Plant delivery options are available for customers who want their selected plants brought directly to their location. Contact Green Haven Nursery to discuss available delivery options and requirements.

Yes. We can help you select suitable office plants for desks, reception areas, meeting rooms, and larger workspaces. Plant choices can be matched with the available lighting and space.

Yes. Our garden maintenance service can help with regular plant care, pruning, general garden upkeep, and maintaining healthy and attractive outdoor spaces.

Green Haven Nursery provides landscaping support for customers who want to improve the appearance and greenery of their outdoor areas with suitable plants and garden arrangements.

Yes. Plant repotting can help when a plant has outgrown its current container or needs fresh growing material. We can assist with selecting a suitable pot and repotting approach.

Healthy plants need suitable light, proper watering, good drainage, appropriate soil, and regular observation. Remove damaged leaves and adjust care when seasonal conditions change.

Plant pots and containers can be selected to complement different plant sizes and spaces. Ask our team about suitable container options for your chosen plant.

Consider the amount of sunlight, available space, watering routine, plant size, and maintenance level before making a selection. Choosing according to your environment can make plant care easier.

You can contact Green Haven Nursery through the contact options provided on our website. Our team can help with plant selection, plant care questions, gardening services, and other nursery related enquiries.
