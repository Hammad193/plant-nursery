Who We Are

A Nursery Built Around a Love for Living Things

Green Haven Nursery began with a simple idea: make it easier for people to bring healthy, beautiful plants into their homes, offices and outdoor spaces.

What started as a small collection of carefully selected plants has grown into a welcoming nursery where beginners, experienced plant lovers and businesses can all find something special.

We focus on healthy plants, practical advice and a friendly experience from choosing your plant to caring for it at home.

Our Purpose

Helping People Create Spaces That Feel Alive

Plants are more than decoration. They bring texture, character and a sense of calm to the spaces where we live and work.
